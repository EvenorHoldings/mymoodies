# Premium Editorial Product Photography

## Goal
Replace the current plain catalog images with a cohesive, cinematic lifestyle campaign while preserving product identity and all shopping behaviour.

## What will change
- Create a coordinated set of premium editorial scenes featuring diverse South African young professionals, creative entrepreneurs, couples, and friends.
- Use natural light, warm cream interiors, outdoor cafés, luxury apartments, minimal accessories, and authentic expressions.
- Pair each product with a relevant lifestyle scene while keeping the product itself clear and recognizable.
- Update image presentation across product cards, product detail pages, and cart thumbnails.
- Add restrained image zoom on hover where it supports product discovery, without affecting touch interaction or reduced-motion preferences.
- Use accurate, descriptive image alt text instead of the current “plain white background” wording.

## Visual direction
- Quiet, tactile, cinematic photography inspired by premium skincare, technology, and South African retail campaigns.
- Soft daylight, restrained neutral palette, subtle film grain, realistic skin texture, and editorial composition.
- No obvious stock-photo poses, excessive props, artificial smiles, or promotional text baked into images.

## Technical details
- Generate reusable high-resolution campaign scenes, then integrate the existing product imagery so packaging and hardware remain recognizable.
- Store optimized assets in the project asset flow and reference them from the existing product data.
- Preserve all product IDs, prices, cart logic, checkout, order history, and route behaviour.
- Verify desktop and mobile product grids, a product detail page, cart imagery, image loading, and the current build status.
